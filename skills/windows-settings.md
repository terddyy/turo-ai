match: =settings, systemsettings
SKILL: Windows 11 Settings (open with Win+I)
Layout: categories in the left column (System, Bluetooth & devices, Network & internet, Personalization, Apps,
Accounts, Time & language, Gaming, Accessibility, Privacy & security, Windows Update). Options open on the right.
Recipes (category > page > option):
- Dark mode: Personalization > Colors > Choose your mode > Dark. Wallpaper: Personalization > Background.
- Bluetooth device: Bluetooth & devices > Add device. Wi-Fi: Network & internet > Wi-Fi.
- Bigger text/apps: System > Display > Scale. Night light: System > Display > Night light.
- Sound output: System > Sound > Output. Notifications / Do not disturb: System > Notifications.
- Battery saver: System > Power & battery > Energy saver.
- Default browser/apps: Apps > Default apps. Startup apps: Apps > Startup.
- Updates: Windows Update > Check for updates. Language: Time & language > Language & region.
- Bigger mouse pointer: Accessibility > Mouse pointer and touch.
Uninstalling apps and resetting the PC are the user's own clicks.
