Where things usually are (labels and icons vary, confirm in the screenshot):
- New chat: "New chat" or a pencil/compose icon, top-left or at the top of the sidebar.
- Message box: the wide text field at the bottom ("Ask anything", "Message...", "How can I help").
- Attach a file or image: a paperclip or "+" icon at the left of the message box.
- Send: the arrow at the right end of the message box (or Enter). Stop answering: the square that replaces it.
- Voice: a microphone or sound-wave icon in or next to the message box.
- Choose a model: the dropdown showing the model name, near the top or in the message box.
- Web search, deep research, image tools: the tools / "+" menu in the message box.
- Settings, memory, custom instructions, personalization: the profile picture or name, bottom-left or top-right.
- Past chats: the sidebar list; a search icon at its top. Rename/delete a chat: hover it, the "..." menu.
- Under an answer: copy, retry/regenerate, thumbs up/down icons. Edit your message: hover it, the pencil icon.
Teaching good prompts (use action "type" with an example, or "explain"):
- Say who it should be, what you want, the context, and the format: "You are a tutor. Explain X to a beginner
  in 5 bullet points with one example."
- Paste or attach the material; ask for a specific length; ask follow-ups to refine instead of starting over.
- Check important facts: AI can be wrong.
